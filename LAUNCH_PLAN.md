## Risks & Mitigations

### Risk: Low engagement
**Mitigation:** The Datadog + UK Gov findings are newsworthy. If one platform fails, move to next.

### Risk: False positive discovered
**Mitigation:** Be transparent, fix immediately, add regression tests.

### Risk: Maintainers dispute findings
**Mitigation:** All findings are documented with proof. Stand behind the work but be open to discussion.

### Risk: No sponsors
**Mitigation:** Build user base first. Sponsors come after trust is established.
